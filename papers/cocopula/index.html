CoCopula: Conformal Calibration and Copula Aggregation for Reliable Renewable Energy Forecasting

Alireza Moradi1,2  ·  Mathieu Tanneau1,2  ·  Reza Zandehshahvar1,2  ·  Pascal Van Hentenryck1,2
1H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering, Georgia Tech   2NSF AI Institute for Advances in Optimization (AI4OPT)
Preprint · under review · 2026
arXiv PDF Code Demo BibTeX
Overview. Site-level probabilistic forecasts are combined with copula-based aggregation, which captures cross-site dependence and gives a fleet-level predictive distribution. Context-aware conformal prediction (CACP) then calibrates the aggregated intervals using past forecast errors and contextual similarity, without any dedicated fleet-level forecasting model.
TL;DR. System operators often have probabilistic forecasts for each solar or wind site but no forecast for the fleet as a whole. CoCopula builds one: a Gaussian copula aggregates the site forecasts while keeping their dependence, and context-aware conformal prediction calibrates the result, giving reliable and sharp fleet-level intervals.

Abstract

The rapid growth of renewable energy penetration has intensified the need for reliable probabilistic forecasts to support grid operations at aggregated (fleet or system) levels. In practice, however, system operators often lack access to fleet-level probabilistic models and instead rely on site-level forecasts produced by heterogeneous third-party providers. Constructing coherent and calibrated fleet-level probabilistic forecasts from such inputs remains challenging due to complex cross-site dependencies and aggregation-induced miscalibration. Motivated by a real-world operational challenge identified through collaboration with an industry partner, this paper presents a probabilistic aggregation and calibration framework that directly converts site-level probabilistic forecasts into reliable fleet-level forecasts. The paper introduces CoCopula (Conformal Calibration and Copula Aggregation), which integrates Copula-based dependence modeling to capture cross-site correlations followed by Context-Aware Conformal Prediction (CACP) to mitigate miscalibration at the aggregated level. This combination enables dependence-aware aggregation while improving empirical coverage and maintaining sharp prediction intervals. Experiments on large-scale solar generation datasets from three system operators in the United States including MISO, ERCOT, and SPP demonstrate that the proposed CoCopula approach achieves near-nominal empirical coverage and consistently lower Winkler scores than competing aggregation-based baselines across different target coverage levels. Importantly, CoCopula achieves performance comparable to directly calibrated system-level forecasts, demonstrating its practical value when dedicated fleet-level forecasting models are unavailable.

How it works

1Learn the dependence
Map each site's past outcomes through its own forecast CDF and fit a Gaussian copula to these values. Its correlation matrix describes how the forecast errors of the sites move together.
2Aggregate
Sample correlated uniforms from the copula, map them through each site's predictive distribution and sum over the sites. The samples give a fleet-level predictive distribution and its intervals.
3Calibrate
CACP widens or narrows each interval by a weighted conformal quantile of past errors, giving more weight to past hours whose context (sun position, time of day and year, previous-day generation) resembles the hour being forecast.
