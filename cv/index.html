Industry Collaborations

  • Réseau de Transport d’Électricité (RTE), France, TS4PS project (PSERC). Led the reconstruction of multi-year, 5-minute nodal active-power injection time series for the RTE7000 French transmission grid (~7,000 buses) by capacity-proportional disaggregation of public regional data (eco2mix, ENTSO-E). Released the open-source pipeline on GitHub and the dataset (~12.8B rows, 2021–2023) on Hugging Face.
  • U.S. Vertically Integrated Utility, solar fleet forecasting. Developed a calibrated probabilistic aggregation framework that turns site-level solar forecasts from heterogeneous third-party providers into reliable fleet-level forecasts, combining copula-based modeling of cross-site dependence with context-aware conformal prediction (CACP) to reach near-nominal coverage with significantly sharper intervals than uncalibrated aggregation.
